The festival

A room,
a screen,
a weekend.

OVERTURE is a fictional festival built around a simple constraint: six films, one room, and enough time to talk about them.

Small on
purpose.

Large festivals are a logistics problem you attend. A small one is a room you are in. The programme is short so that the same people keep meeting each other between screenings.

There is no industry strand, no red carpet and no awards. There is a screen, a bar, and a weekend.

01

One room

Everyone sees the same films in the same order. That shared sequence is the festival.

02

Three strands

After dark, open horizons, close to home. Three ways a film can stay with you.

03

No awards

Nothing is judged. The conversation after the screening is the only verdict.
